KaraokeSpot helps singers find live karaoke nights at physical bars and venues,
and lets the karaoke host (KJ) running that night manage the lineup.

BROWSING — no account needed
Finding venues, browsing the song catalog, and saving favorites all work
anonymously. Grant location permission to see venues sorted by distance. The
app is seeded with venues in Brevard County, FL, so please allow location or
the venue list may appear empty.

PAYMENTS — real-world services at a physical venue
Payments are for services delivered in person at a bar or venue on the night:
premium placement in that venue's live singing queue, and tips paid directly to
the human KJ hosting the event. Nothing digital is unlocked in the app by
paying. These are handled by Stripe Checkout under guideline 3.1.3(e)
(goods and services consumed outside the app) and 3.1.5(b) (person-to-person
services). Funds go to the KJ's own connected Stripe account, less a platform
fee.

IMPORTANT: this build is connected to LIVE Stripe keys, so completing a
checkout results in a real charge to the card used. Please contact us at the
support email before testing a payment and we will arrange a test venue or
refund any review charges immediately.

KJ FEATURES — requires SMS verification
The "I'm the KJ" flow on the Add Spot tab verifies ownership of a phone number
by SMS, so it needs a phone that can receive text messages. If you need to
review the KJ and payout screens, contact us at the support email and we will
supply a verification path.
