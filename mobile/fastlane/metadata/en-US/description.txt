KaraokeSpot is your karaoke companion — find the night, pick the song, take the mic.

🎵 FIND KARAOKE NEAR YOU
Discover karaoke nights at venues near you with location-based search. See who's hosting, what time it starts, and which nights they run.

🎤 SONG SUGGESTIONS FOR YOUR VOICE
Enter your vocal range (bass, baritone, tenor, alto, mezzo, soprano) and your favorite artists. KaraokeSpot's matching suggests songs that fit your voice and style — so you always sound your best.

📣 LIVE AT THE EVENT
When the night is on, the Event tab comes alive. Get in line for your turn and see what's happening — all from your phone.

⚡ MOVE UP IN THE QUEUE
Want to sing sooner? Pay your KJ (karaoke host) directly through the app to move up the line. Secure Stripe checkout, no cash needed. You can tip your KJ through the app too.

⭐ BUILD YOUR SONGBOOK
Search a catalog of tens of thousands of karaoke tracks by title or artist, save the ones you love, and keep your favorites a tap away. No login required to browse and sing.

🎚️ ARE YOU A KJ?
Add your venue, onboard in a few taps, and get paid for premium slots and tips straight to your bank through Stripe. Manage your nights and message your singers from the app.
